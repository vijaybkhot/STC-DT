# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The STC-DT community is committed to a professional, inclusive, and respectful environment for everyone participating in the project, regardless of background, identity, experience, or institutional affiliation.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate constructively and respectfully;
- welcome questions and differing technical perspectives;
- give and receive feedback in good faith;
- acknowledge contributions and sources accurately;
- respect privacy, confidentiality, and research constraints; and
- focus disagreements on ideas, evidence, and project needs.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, unwanted disclosure of private information, and misrepresentation of another person's work or contribution.

## Scope

This code applies in repository activity and in other project spaces or events where an individual is representing STC-DT.

## Reporting and enforcement

Formal ecosystem conduct-reporting and enforcement arrangements are still being established. This document does not assign enforcement authority. This governance work does not alter the policies or reporting processes maintained by individual component repositories.

Do not disclose sensitive personal information in a public issue. Until a private ecosystem reporting channel is published, a participant may open a non-sensitive issue requesting private contact instructions without describing the incident publicly.

Reporting procedures, confidentiality expectations, decision authority, and any appeal process will be documented when ecosystem governance is formally adopted. Until then, no person or group is identified by this document as the ecosystem enforcement authority.
